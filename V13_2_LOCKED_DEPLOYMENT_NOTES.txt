ARL V13.2 LOCKED DEPLOYMENT DESIGN

1. MODERATOR/HOST COMPUTER
   - Full ARL V13.2 Managed Runtime package only.
   - 1_SETUP_THIS_COMPUTER.bat prepares the ARL Python runtime.
   - 2_START_ARL.bat runs the ARL server.
   - 3_CONVERT_RECORDED_XDF.bat runs post-session conversion.

2. PARTICIPANT COMPUTERS P1-P5
   - No ARL Python/runtime/server installation.
   - Use only the lightweight assigned browser launcher.

3. OBSERVER COMPUTER
   - No ARL Python/runtime/server installation.
   - Use Launch_ARL_Observer.bat.
   - Observer is read-only.
   - Observer shows participant station status plus LSL device streams discovered by the ARL host.
   - Observer does not record streams and does not control devices.

4. LABRECORDER
   - Remains the master LSL/XDF recorder.
   - Observer monitoring does not replace the LabRecorder pre-recording verification.

5. DEVICE SOFTWARE
   - Consensys Pro, EMOTIV PRO, LabRecorder, and vendor drivers remain separate and are not installed by the ARL setup BAT.
